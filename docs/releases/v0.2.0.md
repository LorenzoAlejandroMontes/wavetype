Wavetype now runs on Mac.

## Mac

Download **`Wavetype-Mac.dmg`** below, drag Wavetype onto Applications and open it. Apple silicon (M1 and newer), macOS 14 Sonoma or later.

- Tap **Fn** in any app, talk, tap again: clean, punctuated text lands where your cursor is
- Hold **Fn** instead and it listens only while you hold it
- **Ctrl+Option** does the same on keyboards without an Apple Fn key
- Live words on a card next to your caret while you speak
- The first launch walks you through Microphone and Accessibility, each with a live status and a button that opens the right page of System Settings
- Works with a free Groq key, or with the built-in offline engine if you skip the key
- Signed with a Developer ID and notarized by Apple, so it opens with a double click

## Windows

Download **`Wavetype-Setup.exe`** below. Already on 0.1.x? Run the new installer over it: your key and settings stay.

- Builds made with `-WithLocal` now include the voice-detection model the offline engine needs
