Cleaner text from the way you actually talk, and a paste that lands every time.

## Windows and Mac

- **The cleanup pass really cleans.** Fillers, repetitions and false starts go, sound-alike words get fixed, and what you said stays: nothing is summarised
- **Long dictations get more thought.** From 40 words up, the cleanup runs at a higher reasoning effort
- **A busy day on Groq still gives you clean text.** When the main model is rate limited, the cleanup moves to a second model
- **Teach it your words.** List the names and terms the recognizer mishears in `fmt_hints.txt` and the cleanup writes them the way you do

## Windows

Download **`Wavetype-Setup.exe`** below. Already on an earlier version? Run the new installer over it: your key and settings stay.

- **Your dictation is what gets pasted, even with the PC under load.** Wavetype checks the clipboard before pressing Ctrl+V, waits for the target window, and gives your previous clipboard back four seconds later
- **Edit Mode: Grammar and Slack rewrite.** They drop spoken filler and rephrase for the reader; facts, names and numbers stay as you said them

## Mac

Download **`Wavetype-Mac.dmg`** below, drag Wavetype onto Applications and replace the old one. Apple silicon (M1 and newer), macOS 14 Sonoma or later. Signed with a Developer ID and notarized by Apple.
